## What a 2035 grid under each rule would change in the reduced network

Both rules change ratings on 380 kV lines inside the Netherlands and add one radial spur (Rilland-Tilburg) and one short extra circuit; neither changes a cross-border element. With the Netherlands as one node, the equivalent reactances of the 19 branches move only through the extra circuit and the Tilburg spur (tiny); the option-3 limits move where a re-rated line is the binding element of a pair limit (today the Dutch binding lines are Maasbracht-Van Eyck ties, not in either list) or an outage of a re-rated line matters. The difference between the rules is six projects: five reinforcements (Dodewaard-Doetinchem, Maasbracht-Boxmeer-Dodewaard, Geertruidenberg-Rilland) and stations, plus the third Eemshaven circuit.
